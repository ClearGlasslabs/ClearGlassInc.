## Featured Products

### CLEARGLASS FACTCHECK AI

Evidence intelligence platform for transparent claim verification.

Capabilities:

- Automated claim extraction from text, URLs, documents, media, and transcripts
- Multi-source evidence retrieval and source trust scoring
- Provenance records for every evidence item
- Explainable AI reasoning with confidence and limitations
- Evidence graphs connecting claims, sources, contradictions, and verification history
- Governed AI classification separating facts, estimates, assumptions, and recommendations

## ClearGlassInc.

ClearGlassInc. is the engineering repository for a high-assurance technology platform built around secure, bounded, authorized, auditable, production-grade execution.

The platform combines software engineering, AI orchestration, cybersecurity controls, automation, observability, and resilient infrastructure.

## Core engineering principles

- Security by design
- Governed AI
- Operational resilience
- Observability
- Controlled delivery
- Durable architecture
