# Seven-Day Security & Automation Service Sprint

**Status:** Planned, not evidence of completed work. Founder-led; minimal spend.

1. **Day 1 — Baseline:** confirm target segment and offer; inspect live site, contact route, pipeline, payment setup and existing delivery assets; record evidence and blockers.
2. **Day 2 — Scope:** finalize included checks, exclusions, customer prerequisites, authorized access method, report format and realistic delivery window; review the synthetic sample.
3. **Day 3 — Governance:** complete agreement, confidentiality, retention/deletion, AI-tool and escalation drafts. Obtain appropriate review before customer use.
4. **Day 4 — Landing page:** check mobile layout, keyboard navigation, contrast, links, contact route, approved price, terms and privacy links. Publication requires approval.
5. **Day 5 — Pipeline:** identify 10 candidate organizations through appropriate sources or relationships; record fit, source/date and lawful contact context. No bulk outreach.
6. **Day 6 — Discovery:** record only conversations that occur; draft proposals for qualified prospects and obtain approval before sending.
7. **Day 7 — Revenue truth:** reconcile actual enquiries, proposals, agreements, payment-provider records, refunds and delivery evidence. Separate contracted value, invoices, cash collected and recognized revenue.

Success requires evidence, not checklist completion. Never invent prospect contact, meetings, customer outcomes, payment status or revenue. If no paid engagement results, record the evidence and revise the offer.
