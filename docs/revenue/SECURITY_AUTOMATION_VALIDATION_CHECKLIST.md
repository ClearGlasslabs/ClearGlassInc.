# Security & Automation Readiness — Validation Checklist

**Unchecked until verified with evidence.**

## Offer and delivery
- [ ] Target segment and buyer approved.
- [ ] Included checks, exclusions, prerequisites, access method, delivery window and report format finalized.
- [ ] Price, tax, deposit/balance and refund/cancellation terms approved by owner.
- [ ] Payment destination verified and payment status can be checked from provider records.
- [ ] Agreement and data terms reviewed for the actual engagement.
- [ ] Sample report visibly marked synthetic; real report includes sources, dates, limitations and human review.

## Privacy and website
- [ ] Actual storage, access, retention/deletion, AI tools and incident contact verified.
- [ ] No secrets in prompts, reports, logs, source control or public issues.
- [ ] Landing page reviewed on mobile and keyboard; links and contact route tested.
- [ ] Privacy and terms links point to reviewed pages.
- [ ] Price and payment terms match the approved offer.
- [ ] Marketing contact rules and opt-out process reviewed.
- [ ] Pull request checks pass and diff is reviewed; publication separately approved.

## Revenue evidence
- [ ] Prospect records have source/date and fit rationale.
- [ ] Meetings, proposals and agreements reflect real events.
- [ ] Cash collected reconciles to payment provider records.
- [ ] Refunds, fees and taxes tracked appropriately.
- [ ] Delivery and customer acceptance have evidence.
- [ ] Contracted value, invoices, cash collected and recognized revenue are separate.

## Decision
- [ ] Continue: demand and safe delivery supported by evidence.
- [ ] Revise: feedback supports changed offer, segment or price.
- [ ] Pause: authorization, privacy, legal or capacity gate unresolved.
- [ ] Stop: risk exceeds capability or demand evidence is absent.
