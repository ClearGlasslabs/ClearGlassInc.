# Security & Automation Readiness — GTM Campaign

**Status:** Campaign plan only. No prospects researched or contacted by this document.

## Hypothesis
A small professional-services firm may pay for a clearly scoped review if it receives a useful written action plan, has a named buyer, and can approve access and budget. Validate this before expanding the offer.

## First-week target
Build a list of 10 well-qualified organizations; seek 3 appropriate discovery conversations; prepare proposals only where a real need and authority are confirmed. These are experiment targets, not forecasts or completed results.

## Channels
1. Warm introductions through existing relationships.
2. Relevant professional networks and local business events.
3. Helpful original content demonstrating common control/process questions and a synthetic sample report.

## Qualification fields
Organization and segment; source/date; reason for fit; buyer role (verified or unknown); stated problem; urgency; decision authority; procurement constraints; communication context/permission; next step; state; owner.

## Discovery questions
- Which systems are most important to daily operations?
- How are access changes and recovery practices currently handled?
- Which administrative/security process is unclear or time-consuming?
- What happens if it remains unresolved this quarter?
- Who approves this kind of work and what procurement requirements apply?
- What deliverable would make a bounded review worth paying for?
- What would cause you to decline?

## Message draft for appropriate, approved contacts
“I’m validating a focused ClearGlass service for small professional-services teams that want a clearer view of operational security and repetitive workflows. The review is deliberately scoped: we agree on systems and checks up front, then provide a written, prioritized action plan. I’m speaking with business owners and operations leads to understand which problems are most pressing. Would a short conversation about how your team currently handles this be useful?”

Use only when the communication context and applicable rules permit. Record the lawful contact basis and honour opt-outs. No bulk email or automated outbound sequence is authorized.

## Funnel and evidence
Track qualified prospects, two-way conversations, meetings actually held, proposals actually sent, accepted agreements, provider-verified cash, delivery acceptance and follow-up. Preserve the source record for each. Keep estimates and pipeline value separate from actuals.

## Stop / revise conditions
Pause if the buyer's need is unverified, scope exceeds capability, authorization is missing, privacy requirements cannot be met, or customer data handling is not ready. Revise the segment, deliverable or price only from documented feedback.
