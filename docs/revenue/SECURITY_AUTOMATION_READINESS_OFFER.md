# ClearGlass Security & Automation Readiness Review

**Status:** Draft for owner approval; not an approved customer offer.

## Customer and buyer
Initial hypothesis: Ontario professional-services organizations with 10–50 employees; buyer is owner, managing partner, COO, or operations lead. Validate demand before treating this segment as proven.

## Scope
A written review of up to two mutually agreed systems or workflows, subject to authorization and available evidence. Possible checks include access/MFA evidence, account lifecycle, recovery/backup procedures, SaaS ownership, and one repetitive workflow. Only checks listed in the signed scope are included.

## Deliverables
1. Kickoff and written scope confirmation.
2. Evidence register with source, timestamp, limitations, and reviewer.
3. Findings distinguishing observation, inference, and recommendation.
4. Prioritized action plan and closeout discussion.
5. Optional separately scoped implementation proposal.

## Prerequisites
- Named authorized customer contact and written authorization for each system/method.
- Least-privilege, time-limited access through an agreed secure method.
- Confirmed systems, checks, exclusions, customer responsibilities, and escalation contact.
- Signed/accepted scope and reviewed data-handling terms before collection.
- No passwords, MFA codes, private keys, or unnecessary personal data shared by email/chat.

## Boundaries and exclusions
Default to read-only review. No exploitation, penetration testing, phishing, production changes, autonomous remediation, incident response, 24/7 monitoring, formal certification, legal opinion, guaranteed compliance, or guaranteed breach prevention unless separately scoped and appropriately qualified. Stop if authorization or safe scope is unclear.

## Delivery and report
Delivery window is confirmed in the proposal after scope and prerequisites are checked. Report sections: executive summary; scope/exclusions; systems and evidence reviewed; observations; priority/rationale; limitations; recommendations; action owner/date if agreed; sources; reviewer and report date.

## Owner input required before sale
- Exact owner-approved price and currency/tax treatment: **NOT PROVIDED**.
- Deposit, balance due, cancellation/refund policy: **NOT PROVIDED**.
- Payment provider and verified owner-controlled destination: **NOT PROVIDED**.
- Privacy notice, data-retention period, actual storage and AI/subprocessor terms: must be verified.
- Do not create or publish a payment link or quote until these are approved.

## Change control
Additional systems, checks, or remediation require a written change order. A report is complete when agreed observations and limitations are documented and reviewed; it does not imply that every risk has been found or fixed.

## Pre-sale gate
- [ ] Owner approves final scope and price/payment/refund terms.
- [ ] Synthetic report reviewed and visibly marked illustrative.
- [ ] Agreement/data terms reviewed for the actual engagement.
- [ ] Delivery capacity, secure access, and escalation process tested.
- [ ] Contact/payment journey verified.
