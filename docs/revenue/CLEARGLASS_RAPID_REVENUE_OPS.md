# CLEARGLASS RAPID REVENUE OPS

## Mission

Create and operate a truthful, low-cost, 24-hour client-acquisition system for ClearGlass Inc. The immediate objective is to sell a clearly scoped technical service and collect legitimate customer deposits through an owner-approved Stripe or PayPal payment link.

This is **not** a trading, investment, automated-execution, crypto, gambling, advertising-spend, or financial-advice system.

## Primary Offer

**Offer name:**

> ClearGlass 24-Hour Website & GitHub Deployment Rescue

**Core deliverable:**

> I diagnose and repair one defined website, GitHub Pages, deployment, mobile responsiveness, contact form, basic performance, or checkout-link problem within 24 hours after scope confirmation.

## Truth and Safety Rules

- Never promise income, leads, sales, rankings, business growth, uptime, conversion rates, or results that cannot be verified.
- Never claim affiliation with Goldman Sachs, Stripe, PayPal, Etsy, GitHub, OpenAI, or any other third party.
- Never use false testimonials, fake client logos, fake scarcity, fake counts, fabricated case studies, or invented credentials.
- Never send spam, use purchased lead lists, impersonate people, scrape protected data, evade platform rules, or contact people who have opted out.
- Never create or send a payment request, invoice, refund, listing, paid advertisement, email campaign, direct-message campaign, social post, or external communication without explicit owner approval.
- Never access a client's repository, hosting, domain, payment account, or credentials until the client provides authorized access through an approved secure method.
- Never request bank passwords, one-time passcodes, full payment-card details, seed phrases, government IDs, or unnecessary customer information.
- Never modify a client's live website, GitHub repository, DNS, hosting, payment flow, or data without documented scope approval and a backup/rollback plan.
- Never accept work outside ClearGlass's capability or delivery window. State `UNKNOWN`, `BLOCKED`, or `NEEDS-REVIEW` when appropriate.

## Authorization Gates

The system may prepare research, drafts, checklists, scope records, and internal execution plans without external commitment. It must stop before any external communication, payment request, customer-account access, or production/client-system modification unless the owner has explicitly approved that action and the required authorization/evidence is present.

### Customer-work gate

Before client-system access or modification, require:

1. Defined scope.
2. Customer authorization.
3. Approved secure access method.
4. Backup/rollback plan appropriate to the change.
5. Evidence capture before the change.
6. Explicit record of the approved deliverable.

### Payment gate

A Stripe or PayPal payment link may be used only when:

1. The owner has approved the exact offer and price.
2. The payment destination is an owner-controlled, verified account.
3. The customer-facing terms accurately describe the deliverable.
4. No unsupported guarantee or outcome claim is present.
5. The actual payment link is verified before publication or transmission.
6. The owner explicitly approves the external payment request.

## Evidence and State Model

Every acquisition or delivery action should produce auditable evidence where practical:

`intent -> owner approval -> scope -> authorized action -> evidence -> outcome -> customer handoff`

Use these states when evidence is insufficient:

- `UNKNOWN` — required information is not known.
- `BLOCKED` — an authorization, credential, dependency, or policy gate prevents execution.
- `INCOMPLETE` — the specification or evidence is missing required elements.
- `NEEDS-REVIEW` — human judgment or approval is required before proceeding.
- `VERIFIED` — the claimed state is supported by concrete evidence.
